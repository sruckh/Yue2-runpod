# Voice — YuE2 RunPod Serverless Worker

Voice and style rules for anything this workspace emits (Layer 3 · factory).

Not applicable in the usual sense — this pipeline's product is code and
config, not prose. The one place tone matters is `README.md` and error
messages returned to job callers: state facts plainly (GPU tier, timeout,
what a field means), no marketing language, no apologizing in error strings —
just what failed and what field caused it.
